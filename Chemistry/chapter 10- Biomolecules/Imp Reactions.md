# Most Important Reactions to Memorise

### Glucose + Bromine Water

$Glucose\xrightarrow{Br_2/H_2O}Gluconic\ acid$

Shows:

$-CHO$

---

### Glucose + Nitric Acid

$Glucose\xrightarrow{HNO_3}Saccharic\ acid$

Oxidises both terminal groups.

---

### Glucose + Hydroxylamine

$Glucose+NH_2OH\rightarrow Glucose\ oxime+H_2O$

Shows the presence of a carbonyl group.

---

### Glucose + Acetic Anhydride

$Glucose+5(CH_3CO)_2O\rightarrow Glucose\ pentaacetate+5CH_3COOH$

Shows the presence of:

$5OH$

groups.

---

### Glucose + HI

$Glucose\xrightarrow[\text{heat}]{HI}n\text{-hexane}$

Shows a six-carbon straight chain.

---

### Sucrose Hydrolysis

$Sucrose+H_2O\rightarrow Glucose+Fructose$

---

### Starch Hydrolysis

$Starch+H_2O\xrightarrow{H^+}Glucose$

---

### Peptide Formation

$Amino\ acid+Amino\ acid\rightarrow Dipeptide+H_2O$
