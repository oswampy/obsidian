# Vitamins

Vitamins are organic compounds required in **small quantities** for normal growth and functioning.

Most vitamins must be obtained from the diet.

---

# Classification of Vitamins

### Fat-soluble vitamins

$A,D,E,K$

### Water-soluble vitamins

$B$-complex and $C$

---

# Important Vitamins

|Vitamin|Important source|Deficiency|
|---|---|---|
|A|Fish liver oil, carrots, leafy vegetables|Night blindness, xerophthalmia|
|B₁|Whole grains, yeast|Beriberi|
|B₂|Milk, eggs|Skin/mucosal problems|
|B₆|Cereals, meat|Anaemia, dermatitis|
|B₁₂|Meat, milk, eggs|Pernicious anaemia|
|C|Citrus fruits, amla|Scurvy|
|D|Sunlight, fish liver oil|Rickets/osteomalacia|
|E|Vegetable oils, nuts|Reproductive/neuromuscular effects|
|K|Green leafy vegetables|Delayed blood clotting|

---

# Vitamin A

Important for:

- Vision
    
- Growth
    
- Maintenance of epithelial tissues
    

Deficiency:

**Night blindness**

Severe deficiency can cause xerophthalmia.

---

# Vitamin B₁

Chemical name:

**Thiamine**

Deficiency:

**Beriberi**

---

# Vitamin B₂

Chemical name:

**Riboflavin**

Deficiency can cause inflammation of the mouth and skin problems.

---

# Vitamin B₆

Chemical name:

**Pyridoxine**

Deficiency can cause:

- Dermatitis
    
- Anaemia
    
- Neurological effects
    

---

# Vitamin B₁₂

Chemical name:

**Cyanocobalamin**

Deficiency:

**Pernicious anaemia**

---

# Vitamin C

Chemical name:

**Ascorbic acid**

Sources:

- Citrus fruits
    
- Amla
    
- Tomatoes
    
- Green vegetables
    

Deficiency:

**Scurvy**

---

# Vitamin D

Vitamin D is important for:

- Calcium absorption
    
- Bone formation
    

Deficiency:

**Rickets in children**

Vitamin D can be formed in the skin under sunlight.

---

# Vitamin E

Vitamin E mainly acts as an antioxidant.

Sources include:

- Vegetable oils
    
- Nuts
    
- Seeds
    

---

# Vitamin K

Vitamin K is essential for normal:

**Blood clotting**

Deficiency causes:

**Delayed blood clotting**
