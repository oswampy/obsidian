# Formula Sheet — Solutions

### Henry's law

$p=K_Hx$

---

### Raoult's law

$p_i=x_ip_i^0$

$p_{total}=x_1p_1^0+x_2p_2^0$

---

### Relative lowering of vapour pressure

$\frac{\Delta p}{p^0}=x_2$

For abnormal behaviour:

$\frac{\Delta p}{p^0}=ix_2$

---

### Elevation of boiling point

$\Delta T_b=K_bm$

With van't Hoff factor:

$\boxed{\Delta T_b=iK_bm}$

---

### Depression of freezing point

$\Delta T_f=K_fm$

With van't Hoff factor:

$\boxed{\Delta T_f=iK_fm}$

---

### Osmotic pressure

$\pi=CRT$

$\pi=\frac{nRT}{V}$

With van't Hoff factor:

$\boxed{\pi=iCRT}$

---

### Van't Hoff factor

$i=\frac{\text{Observed colligative property}}{\text{Calculated colligative property}}$

$i=\frac{M_{normal}}{M_{observed}}$

---

### Dissociation

$i=1+\alpha(\nu-1)$

$\alpha=\frac{i-1}{\nu-1}$

---

### Association

$i=1-\alpha+\frac{\alpha}{n}$

---

### Dimerisation

$i=1-\frac{\alpha}{2}$

---

### Dilution

$\boxed{M_1V_1=M_2V_2}$

---

# 🔥 JEE Must-Know Points

### Remember these relationships

**Higher $K_H$ → lower gas solubility**

$K_H\uparrow\Rightarrow x\downarrow$

---

**Positive deviation:**

$A-B$ weaker

$\Delta H_{mix}>0$

Vapour pressure $\uparrow$

Boiling point $\downarrow$

---

**Negative deviation:**

$A-B$ stronger

$\Delta H_{mix}<0$

Vapour pressure $\downarrow$

Boiling point $\uparrow$

---

**Dissociation:**

Number of particles $\uparrow$

$i>1$

$M_{observed}<M_{normal}$

---

**Association:**

Number of particles $\downarrow$

$i<1$

$M_{observed}>M_{normal}$

---

**No association/dissociation:**

$i=1$

---

**Molarity:**

Uses **volume of solution**

---

**Molality:**

Uses **mass of solvent**

---

**Osmotic pressure:**

$\pi=CRT$

and is particularly useful for finding molar masses of **macromolecules**.
