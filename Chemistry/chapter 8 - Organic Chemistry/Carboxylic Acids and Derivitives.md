# CARBOXYLIC ACIDS (Page 1)

## Methods of Preparation

### 1) From Oxidation of $1^\circ$ Alcohols

$$\text{R}-\text{CH}_2-\text{OH} \xrightarrow{[\text{O}]} \text{R}-\underset{\substack{\vert \\ \text{H}}}{\text{C}}(\text{OH})_2 \longrightarrow \text{R}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{H} \xrightarrow{[\text{O}]} \text{R}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{OH}$$

#### S.O.A (Strong Oxidizing Agents)

1. $\text{KMnO}_4 \,/\, \text{H}^+$ or $\text{KMnO}_4 \,/\, \Delta$
    
2. $\text{K}_2\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_7 \,/\, \text{H}^+$ or $\text{hot } \text{K}_2\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_7$
    
3. $\text{conc. } \text{HNO}_3 \,/\, \Delta$
    
4. $\text{CrO}_3 + \text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ (**Jones' Reagent**)
    

### 2) Popoff's Rule :- (Strong Oxidizing Agents)

$$\text{2-Methylcyclohexanone} \rightleftharpoons \text{Enol Form} \longrightarrow \text{HOOC}-(\text{CH}_2)_4-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{CH}_3$$

_(The C-C bond cleaves next to the carbonyl group, keeping the keto group with the smaller alkyl segment if applicable)._

### 3) Side Chain Oxidation :-

- **Condition:** At least $1$ '$\text{H}$' is present ($\alpha\text{-H}$) at the benzylic position.
    

$$\text{C}_6\text{H}_5-\text{R} \xrightarrow[\Delta]{\text{KMnO}_4 \,/\, \text{KOH}} \text{C}_6\text{H}_5-\text{COOK} \xrightarrow{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+} \text{C}_6\text{H}_5-\text{COOH}$$

#### Example:

$$\text{C}_6\text{H}_5-\text{CH}_3 \,(3\alpha\,\text{H}) \xrightarrow[\text{H}_3\text{O}^+]{\text{KMnO}_4 \,/\, \text{KOH}, \,\Delta} \text{C}_6\text{H}_5-\text{COOH}$$

### Examples (Side Chain Oxidation continued):

1. **Ethylbenzene to Benzoic Acid:**
    
    $$\text{C}_6\text{H}_5-\text{CH}_2\text{CH}_3 \longrightarrow \text{C}_6\text{H}_5-\text{COOH}$$
    
2. **Isopropylbenzene (Cumene) to Benzoic Acid:**
    
    $$\text{C}_6\text{H}_5-\text{CH}(\text{CH}_3)_2 \longrightarrow \text{C}_6\text{H}_5-\text{COOH}$$
    
3. **1,2-Dimethylbenzene (o-Xylene) to Phthalic Acid:**
    
    $$o\text{-}\text{C}_6\text{H}_4(\text{CH}_3)_2 \longrightarrow o\text{-}\text{C}_6\text{H}_4(\text{COOH})_2$$
    
4. **1-Ethyl-2-methylbenzene to Phthalic Acid:**
    
    $$o\text{-}\text{C}_6\text{H}_4(\text{CH}_3)(\text{CH}_2\text{CH}_3) \longrightarrow o\text{-}\text{C}_6\text{H}_4(\text{COOH})_2$$
    
5. **1,2,4-Trimethylbenzene to Trimellitic Acid:**
    
    $$1,2,4\text{-}\text{C}_6\text{H}_3(\text{CH}_3)_3 \longrightarrow 1,2,4\text{-}\text{C}_6\text{H}_3(\text{COOH})_3$$
    
6. **Tetralin (1,2,3,4-Tetrahydronaphthalene) to Phthalic Acid:**
    
    $$\text{C}_{10}\text{H}_{12} \longrightarrow o\text{-}\text{C}_6\text{H}_4(\text{COOH})_2$$
    
7. **Anthracene derivative (Oxidation of terminal rings):**
    
    $$\text{Anthracene core system} \longrightarrow \text{Benzene-1,2,4,5-tetracarboxylic acid} \begin{bmatrix} \text{HOOC} & & \text{COOH} \\ & \text{C}_6\text{H}_2 & \\ \text{HOOC} & & \text{COOH} \end{bmatrix}$$
    

### Important Notes (NB):

1. **tert-Butylbenzene (No benzylic $\alpha\text{-H}$):**
    
    $$\text{C}_6\text{H}_5-\text{C}(\text{CH}_3)_3 \longrightarrow \mathbf{\text{No reaction}}$$
    
2. **Naphthalene with an Electron Donating Group ($+\text{M}$):**
    
    $$\text{Ring with }+\text{M}\text{ group is activated} \longrightarrow \text{The opposite ring gets oxidized} \longrightarrow \text{Phthalic Acid } [o\text{-}\text{C}_6\text{H}_4(\text{COOH})_2]$$
    
3. **Naphthalene with an Electron Withdrawing Group ($-\text{M}$):**
    
    $$\text{Ring with }-\text{M}\text{ group is deactivated} \longrightarrow \text{The ring containing }-\text{M}\text{ survives, outer ring is oxidized} \longrightarrow 3\text{-Nitrophthalic acid (or corresponding substituted derivative)}$$

### 4) From Grignard Reagent :-

$$\text{R}-\text{MgX} + \text{O}=\text{C}=\text{O} \longrightarrow \text{R}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{O}^-\text{MgX}^+ \xrightarrow{\text{H}_2\text{O}} \text{R}-\text{COOH}$$

#### Question:

$$\text{Ph}-\text{MgBr} \xrightarrow{1)\,\text{CO}_2} \text{Ph}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{O}^-\text{MgBr}^+ \xrightarrow{2)\,\text{H}_2\text{O}} \text{Ph}-\text{COOH} \quad (\text{Benzoic Acid})$$

### 5) From Hydrolysis of Cyanide and Acid Derivatives :-

#### Partial Hydrolysis of Cyanide:

$$\text{R}-\text{C}\equiv\text{N} \xrightarrow[\text{Partial}]{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+} \left[ \text{R}-\underset{\substack{\vert \\ \text{OH}}}{\text{C}}=\text{NH} \right] \longrightarrow \text{R}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{NH}_2 \quad (\text{Amide})$$

#### Complete Hydrolysis of Cyanide:

$$\text{R}-\text{C}\equiv\text{N} \xrightarrow{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+} \left[ \text{R}-\underset{\substack{\vert \\ \text{OH}}}{\text{C}}(\text{OH})_2 \right] \longrightarrow \text{R}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{OH} \quad (\text{Carboxylic Acid})$$

#### General Hydrolysis of Acid Derivatives ($\text{R-CO-Z}$):

$$\text{R}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{Z} \xrightarrow{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+} \text{R}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{OH} + \text{H-Z}$$

- **For Acid Chloride ($\text{Z} = \text{Cl}$):**
    
    $$\text{R}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{Cl} \xrightarrow{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+} \text{R}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{OH} + \text{HCl}$$
    
- **For Acid Anhydride ($\text{Z} = \text{OCOR}'$):**
    
    $$\text{R}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{O}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{R}' \xrightarrow{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+} \text{R}-\text{COOH} + \text{R}'-\text{COOH}$$
    
- **For Ester ($\text{Z} = \text{OR}'$):**
    
    $$\text{R}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{OR}' \xrightarrow{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+} \text{R}-\text{COOH} + \text{R}'-\text{OH}$$
    
- **For Amide ($\text{Z} = \text{NR}_2$):**
    
    $$\text{R}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{NR}_2 \xrightarrow{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+} \text{R}-\text{COOH} + \text{R}_2\text{NH}$$
    

#### Alkaline Hydrolysis of Ester (Saponification):

$$\text{R}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{OR}' \xrightarrow{\text{Alkaline} \;\; \text{H}_2\text{O}} \text{R}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{O}^- + \text{R}'\text{OH}$$

### 6) From Oxidative Ozonolysis of Alkenes and Alkynes :-

#### Alkenes:

$$\begin{matrix} \text{R}_1 \\ \text{R}_2 \end{matrix} \text{C}=\text{CH}-\text{R}_3 \longrightarrow \begin{matrix} \text{R}_1 \\ \text{R}_2 \end{matrix}\text{C}=\text{O} + \text{O}=\text{C}\begin{matrix} \text{R}_3 \\ \text{OH} \end{matrix}$$

#### Alkynes:

$$\text{R}_1-\text{C}\equiv\text{C}-\text{R}_2 \longrightarrow \text{R}_1-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\overset{\substack{\text{O} \\ \parallel}}{\text{C}}-\text{R}_2 \longrightarrow \text{R}_1\text{COOH} + \text{R}_2\text{COOH}$$

#### Reagents Used for Oxidative Cleavage:

1. $\text{O}_3 + \text{CCl}_4 \,/\, \text{H}_2\text{O}$
    
2. $\text{O}_3 + \text{CCl}_4 \,/\, \text{Ag}_2\text{O}$
    
3. $\text{KMnO}_4 \,/\, \text{H}^+ \text{ or } (\text{KOH})$
    
4. $\text{K}_2\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_7 \,/\, \text{H}^+ \text{ or } (\text{KOH})$

$$\text{R}-\text{COOH} \rightleftharpoons \text{R}-\text{COO}^- + \text{H}^+$$
