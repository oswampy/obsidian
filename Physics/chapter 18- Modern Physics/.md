## 14. Special Semiconductor Devices — JEE Main

### 14.1 Zener Diode

A Zener diode is a specially designed heavily doped p-n junction diode intended to operate in reverse breakdown.

Properties:

- It is designed to work in reverse bias.
    
- It maintains an approximately constant voltage across itself in its breakdown region.
    
- It is used as a voltage regulator.
    

### 14.2 Zener Diode as a Voltage Regulator

A Zener diode is connected in reverse bias across the load.

A series resistor limits the current.

When the input voltage or load current changes within the permitted operating range, the Zener maintains approximately constant output voltage.

$V_{out}\approx V_Z$

### 14.3 LED — Light Emitting Diode

An LED emits light when forward biased.

**Working:**

- Electrons and holes recombine near the junction.
    
- Energy is released in the form of photons.
    
- The colour depends on the semiconductor material and its band gap.
    

Photon energy:

$E_g\approx h\nu=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$

Applications:

- Indicator lights.
    
- Displays.
    
- Lighting systems.
    
- Optical communication.
    

### 14.4 Photodiode

A photodiode is a semiconductor device that converts incident light into electrical current.

It is generally operated in reverse bias.

**Working:**

- Incident photons generate electron-hole pairs.
    
- The electric field in the depletion region separates these carriers.
    
- Reverse current increases with light intensity.
    

Applications:

- Light sensors.
    
- Optical communication.
    
- Automatic lighting systems.
    

### 14.5 Solar Cell

A solar cell converts light energy directly into electrical energy using the photovoltaic effect.

**Working:**

- Light generates electron-hole pairs.
    
- The junction electric field separates the charge carriers.
    
- A potential difference develops across the terminals.
    
- Current can flow through an external circuit.
    

Important points:

- A solar cell operates without an external bias supply.
    
- It is generally a large-area p-n junction.
    
- It converts solar energy into electrical energy.
    

### 14.6 Comparison of LED, Photodiode and Solar Cell

|Device|Main function|Usual operation|
|---|---|---|
|LED|Electrical energy to light|Forward bias|
|Photodiode|Light to electrical signal|Reverse bias|
|Solar cell|Light to electrical energy|Photovoltaic mode|
